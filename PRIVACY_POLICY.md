# Privacy Policy - Home Guardian

Last updated: 27 June 2026

Home Guardian ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Home Guardian Android app (`com.mshomeguardian.logger`) collects, how it is used, and your choices.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on permissions you grant, Home Guardian may collect:

- Device information (model, OS version, identifiers, battery and system status)
- Location data (latitude, longitude, timestamp)
- Call logs and communication metadata
- SMS/MMS message metadata and content
- Contacts data (names, phone numbers, email addresses)
- App usage and installed apps information
- Network usage data
- Sensor data (for example accelerometer, gyroscope, proximity, light, step count)
- Health Connect data that you grant access to (for example heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2, respiratory rate, temperature, height, weight, steps)
- Audio recordings and transcription metadata
- Digital wellbeing usage snapshots (for example total screen time, app launches, unlock count, top app usage)
- Diagnostic and sync metadata

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide monitoring and logging features requested by the user
- Store and sync records to the account owner's cloud storage
- Improve app reliability, diagnostics, and synchronization
- Support app functionality such as transcription and background collection
- Support optional health and wellbeing features when connected to Health Connect

## 3. Data Storage and Sharing

- Data is stored locally on your device and may be synced to Firebase services (Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage) under your authenticated account.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not share your data with third parties for advertising.
- Data may be disclosed only when required by law or to protect legal rights.

## 4. Permissions

Home Guardian requests only permissions required for enabled features. You can deny or revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings, but some features may stop working.

## 5. Data Retention

Data is retained as long as needed for app functionality and account usage, unless you delete it or uninstall the app. Retention may vary by data type and your in-app usage.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit and at rest. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Home Guardian is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Your Choices

You can:

- Uninstall the app at any time
- Revoke app permissions in Android settings
- Request account deletion and data removal at any time

## 8.1. Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, please visit our [Account Deletion page](https://github.com/sadakpramodh/com.mshomeguardian.logger/blob/main/ACCOUNT_DELETION.md) or email sadakpramodh@outlook.com with your deletion request. All personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days of verification.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

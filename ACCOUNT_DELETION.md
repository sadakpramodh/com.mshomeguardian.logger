# Account Deletion & Data Removal

## How to Delete Your Account

If you wish to delete your Home Guardian account and remove all associated data, please follow these instructions:

### Request Account Deletion

Send an email to **sadakpramodh@outlook.com** with the subject line:
```
Account Deletion Request - Home Guardian
```

In your email, please include:
1. The email address associated with your Home Guardian account
2. The device ID(s) you want to delete data for (if known)
3. A brief description confirming you want to delete your account

### What Gets Deleted

Upon verification of your identity, the following will be deleted:

- Your account login credentials
- All collected device monitoring data including:
  - Location history
  - Call logs and SMS messages
  - Contact information
  - Device information and metrics
  - Audio recordings and transcripts
  - App usage statistics
  - Sensor data
  - Network usage records
  - Any other data associated with your account

### Local Device Data

To delete data stored locally on your device:

1. Uninstall the Home Guardian app from your Android device
2. This will remove all local data stored by the app

### Firestore & Cloud Storage

Upon receiving your deletion request and verifying your identity, we will:

1. Remove your user record from Firebase Firestore
2. Delete all associated device records
3. Remove any files stored in Firebase Cloud Storage
4. Permanently erase all backup copies

### Timeline

Account deletion requests are typically processed within **30 days** of receipt. You will receive confirmation via email once the deletion is complete.

### Data Storage During Processing

During the processing period (up to 30 days), your data may still be accessible in our backup systems. After the 30-day period, all data will be permanently deleted.

### Privacy Assurance

- We will not use your data for any purpose after you request deletion
- Your email address will only be used to confirm your deletion request and notify you of completion
- We do not share your information with third parties
